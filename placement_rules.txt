Eligibility: A student must have a minimum CGPA of 6.0 to sit for any campus placement drive.

Backlogs: Students with more than 1 active backlog cannot sit for Tier-1 drives. Students with no active backlogs are eligible for all drives.

Marks: Minimum 60% or above in both 10th (SSC) and 12th (HSC/Diploma) examinations is mandatory for all campus placement drives.

Tier-1 drives: Tier-1 companies offer packages above 10 LPA. They require a CGPA of 7.5 or above and a strong coding assessment score.

Tier-2 drives: Tier-2 companies offer packages between 5 and 10 LPA. They require a CGPA of 6.5 or above.

Mass recruiters: Mass recruiter drives offer packages up to 5 LPA and are open to all eligible students with a CGPA of 6.0 or above.

Off-campus wins: Sahil Gujrati secured an 80 LPA offer off-campus at Keyring Network.

One offer policy: A student who accepts an offer from a Tier-1 company is not allowed to sit for further drives.

Internships: A completed internship is not mandatory, but it is given preference during shortlisting.
